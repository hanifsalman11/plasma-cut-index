Frequently Asked Questions (Everlast PowerPlasma 50S / 60S / 80S CNC Pilot Arc Settings)

What cut speed (IPM), pierce delay, and kerf width should I use for Everlast PowerPlasma 50S / 60S / 80S CNC Pilot Arc Settings?

For 0.375-inch metal at 60 Amps, program your CAM post-processor (Fusion 360 / SheetCam) to 58 IPM cut speed, 0.5 seconds pierce delay, 0.15" pierce height, 0.06" cut height, 0.068" kerf width, and ~130V THC arc voltage.

What size 240V breaker and wire gauge do I need for a 60A plasma cutter?

At 60A cutting output and ~130V arc voltage (85% inverter efficiency), the machine draws ~9.2 kW (38.3A at 240V). Under NEC Article 630 duty-cycle rules, wire a dedicated 50-Amp 2-pole circuit breaker with 8/2 or 6/2 AWG Copper (NEMA 6-50) to a NEMA 6-50R receptacle.

How much air CFM and PSI does Everlast PowerPlasma 50S / 60S / 80S CNC Pilot Arc Settings require?

A 60A plasma torch requires 6.7 SCFM of continuous clean, dry air regulated to 75 PSI while flowing. Pair your compressor with a 0.01-micron coalescing filter and a color-changing silica desiccant dryer.
